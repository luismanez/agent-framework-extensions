# Meridian Frostline Beacon

> Synthetic test document for Microsoft 365 Retrieval. This product and all details below are fictional.

## Product overview

The Meridian Frostline Beacon is a portable environmental monitor designed for temporary cold-storage facilities. Its internal product code is **MFB-742-C**, and its development codename is **Juniper Comet**.

The beacon measures ambient temperature, relative humidity, and door-open duration. It stores readings locally when a network connection is unavailable and uploads them after connectivity is restored.

## Operating specifications

- Supported temperature range: **-38 C to 52 C**
- Humidity measurement range: **10% to 95% relative humidity**
- Rated battery life: **19 hours** under continuous monitoring
- Full recharge time: **2 hours and 40 minutes**
- Ingress protection rating: **IP67**
- Maximum offline storage: **14,400 readings**

## Calibration procedure

Calibration must be performed every **93 days**.

1. Place the beacon in a stable environment between 18 C and 24 C.
2. Hold the north and south buttons together for **11 seconds**.
3. Wait for the status indicator to flash amber twice.
4. Enter reference code **FROST-93-JC** in the companion application.
5. Keep the beacon stationary for seven minutes while calibration completes.

A double amber flash confirms that calibration mode has started. A steady violet indicator confirms that calibration completed successfully.

## Alert behavior

The beacon raises a critical temperature alert after the measured temperature remains outside the configured range for **six consecutive minutes**. Opening and closing the storage door does not reset this timer.

When a critical alert is active, the beacon sounds three short tones every 30 seconds and displays a pulsing red indicator.
